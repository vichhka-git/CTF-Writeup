## Description & Scenario

An HR employee reported receiving an email from a job applicant named 
**Aurelio Nadeau** containing what looked like a CV and cover letter. The
employee says they simply opened the PDF attachment to review the application.



Minutes later the SOC flagged an unusual outbound connection from the
workstation. The employee insists:



*"I just opened a PDF. I didn't run anything."*



Forensic artifacts have been collected from the workstation. Trace the full
attack chain &mdash; from initial access to exfiltration &mdash; and answer the
questions.
