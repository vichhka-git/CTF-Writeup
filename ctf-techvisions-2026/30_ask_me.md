## 30 — Ask Me (OSINT)

1. Open the eight PNG screenshots in order. The moving diamond sword changes position between frames.
2. In each frame, count the diamond-shaped particles or blocks after the sword. The eight counts are ASCII codes.
3. The ASCII codes decode to `F@OuNdIT`. Use that as the password for `FLAG.7z`.
4. Extract `FLAG.png` and read the flag from the image.
